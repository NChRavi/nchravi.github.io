Required & Recommended Textbooks

Essential literature and reference materials for all 5 university courses.

Required Used in: CS 501, CS 610

Deep Learning

By Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville

ISBN-13: 978-0262035613 • MIT Press (2016)

The definitive textbook on deep learning covering mathematical fundamentals, linear algebra, deep feedforward networks, regularization, optimization, and generative modeling.

Free Online Edition
Required Used in: CS 302

Introduction to Algorithms (4th Edition)

By Thomas H. Cormen, Charles E. Leiserson, Ronald L. Rivest, and Clifford Stein

ISBN-13: 978-0262046305 • MIT Press (2022)

Comprehensive reference guide on algorithms and data structures. Comprehensive coverage of sorting, search trees, graph algorithms, multi-threaded algorithms, and dynamic programming.

Publisher Preview
Recommended Used in: CS 520

Speech and Language Processing (3rd Draft)

By Daniel Jurafsky and James H. Martin

Stanford Draft Edition (2023)

An essential introduction to natural language processing, speech recognition, transformer-based language models, and computational linguistics.

Draft Chapters Online
Required Used in: CS 405

Ethics of Artificial Intelligence

Edited by S. Matthew Liao

ISBN-13: 978-0190905033 • Oxford University Press (2020)

Collection of foundational essays on ethical frameworks, superintelligence risks, automated decision bias, and regulatory approaches to modern AI deployment.

Publisher Information
