Required & Recommended TextBooks

Essential academic references for Dr. N. Ch. Ravi's courses.

Required Used in: CSE 411 (Quantum AI & Machine Learning)

Quantum Computing for Computer Scientists

By Noson S. Yanofsky & Mirco A. Mannucci

ISBN-13: 978-0521879965 • Cambridge University Press

Rigorous mathematical bridge connecting linear algebra, quantum mechanics, logic gates, and quantum algorithm design for computer scientists.

Required Used in: CSE 423 (Web Security & Blockchain)

The Web Application Hacker's Handbook

By Dafydd Stuttard & Marcus Pinto

ISBN-13: 978-1118026472 • Wiley

Definitive technical breakdown of modern web vulnerabilities including SQL injections, cross-site scripting (XSS), and defense mechanics.

Recommended Used in: CSE 423 (Blockchain Specialization)

Blockchain Basics: A Non-Technical Introduction in 25 Steps

By Daniel Drescher

ISBN-13: 978-1484226032 • Apress

Clear, conceptual explanation of cryptographic hashing, consensus algorithms, distributed ledgers, and smart contracts.
